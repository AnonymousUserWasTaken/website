About the Company

Active Fire Protection Services LLC is a trusted fire protection company proudly serving Houston, Texas, and the surrounding areas. Founded on more than 27 years of industry experience, we are committed to delivering reliable, code-compliant fire protection solutions with exceptional customer service and attention to detail. We specialize in fire sprinkler installation, inspections, testing, maintenance, and repairs, as well as fire alarm inspections and service, fire extinguisher inspections and maintenance, and backflow testing and service. We proudly serve commercial, industrial, and residential customers, helping ensure their fire protection systems remain compliant, dependable, and ready when they matter most. As a Houston-based company, we understand the needs of local businesses and property owners. Our efficient business model allows us to provide personalized service, quick response times, and competitive pricing without compromising on quality, safety, or workmanship. Every project is completed with professionalism, integrity, and a commitment to exceeding our customers' expectations. At Active Fire Protection Services LLC, our mission is simple: to protect lives and property by providing dependable fire protection services that our customers can trust for years to come.
